# Assumptions

1. Missing discount percentages are treated as 0%.
2. Duplicate order IDs are considered invalid and removed.
3. Negative sales quantities are treated as erroneous records.
4. Inventory reorder status is determined by comparing stock availability against reorder level.
5. Revenue calculations account for applied discounts.
6. Weekly revenue trends are calculated using weekly aggregation.
7. Return rates are calculated on a category basis.
8. All generated reports are intended for management review purposes.
